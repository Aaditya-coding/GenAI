### **The Game of Cricket**

Under the sun, the stadium roars,
The bat meets ball, and the battle begins once more.
A bowler runs in with fire in his eyes,
While the batsman stands beneath endless skies.

**Four!** — the crowd erupts in cheer,
**Six!** — and the ball disappears.
A fielder dives, a catch flies high,
As dreams and hopes race through the sky.

Every run is a step toward glory,
Every wicket adds a chapter to the story.
From street-side games to stadium lights,
Cricket fills our hearts on countless nights.

When the final ball is bowled at last,
The winners celebrate, the memories last.
For cricket is more than bat and ball—
It's passion, teamwork, and the thrill of it all.
